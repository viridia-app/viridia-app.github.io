What Viridia is

What it is
Not a wellness app. A reference.

Herbs, nutrients, minerals, compounds, and modalities — if it has demonstrable historical efficacy, it's in here. Every remedy comes with preparation method, cautions, body system, and citations. Two warning flags: one for general caution, one specifically for estrogenic activity. Clarity, not curation.

What it isn't
No vague claims. No affiliate supplements.

No "this herb changed my life" without telling you the mechanism. No sponsored products. No empowerment language where plain information would do. No infinite scroll. Just honest information about what a remedy actually does — and a clear warning when you need one.

Healing is not mystical. It is orderly.
Viridia is built from that conviction.
